02 /About

Photo

I'm a senior product designer in Toronto who codes. At Info-Tech Research Group, I design and build the interface for CIO Analytics, an AI-powered analytics platform for IT leaders. Over seven years there, I've also designed the infographics that sum up Info-Tech's research and the diagnostic reports that benchmark IT teams.

Outside of work, I play bass (mostly the metal and punk I listen to), draw graffiti-inspired, abstract and typographic art, and read a lot of horror, usually with a cat or two nearby.
